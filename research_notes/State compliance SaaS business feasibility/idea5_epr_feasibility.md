## 1. Converter willingness to pay: do converters pay for customer-facing sustainability/compliance tools, and do they sell EPR services today?

### Takeaway
After two research rounds there is still **no direct evidence that any US converter pays for a customer-facing EPR tool, or charges brands for EPR data services**. The closest analogs follow one pattern: converters spend on credentials and data hygiene, while the customer-facing EPR content they offer is free and used for marketing. Examples are How2Recycle converter membership with an annual fee, ERP vendors bundling carbon-at-quote estimates through partners, and free brand-led data templates. No survey of US converters' EPR readiness or budgets was found.

### Cited Findings
- **No converter EPR service offers were found.** Targeted searches for EPR data or reporting services at Amcor, ProAmpac, Sonoco, Veritiv, TricorBraun and Berlin Packaging (Oct 2026) returned only corporate news, such as Veritiv selling its rigid-container business to TricorBraun (closing Q1 2025), and Sonoco's statement that converters play a "central role." No paid or free EPR reporting service by these firms surfaced. — [Packaging Strategies (TricorBraun–Veritiv Containers)](https://www.packagingstrategies.com/articles/105269-tricorbraun-acquires-vertiv-containers); [Packaging World (Sonoco)](https://www.packworld.com/sustainable-packaging/recycling/article/22952828/brands-and-converters-align-on-epr-data-demands) (absence of evidence, not proof)
- **Sonoco's EPR contribution is free.** It is a founding member of the Sustainable Packaging Data Council, which "launched a free template for reporting in Oregon." — [Packaging Dive](https://www.packagingdive.com/news/epr-reporting-template-sustainable-packaging-data-council/759186/)
- **Trade framing treats supplier spec data as an obligation, not a product.** Detailed specifications "isn't an optional value-add, it's an essential service that enables brands to calculate fees." — [Packaging World / Healthcare Packaging mirror](https://www.healthcarepackaging.com/home/article/22952828/brands-and-converters-align-on-epr-data-demands) (search excerpt)
- **How2Recycle is the one clear case of converters paying for a customer-facing sustainability credential.** Converters and material manufacturers can join under a signed agreement and "may need to fulfill an annual membership fee, depending on your company type." Members get recyclability feedback and pre-qualified labels, but the brand on the package must itself be a member to use the label. A converter prequalification letter "is not a certification." The fee amount is not public. — [GreenBlue How2Recycle membership](https://greenblue.org/projects/how2recycle/how2recycle-membership/); [Inline Plastics prequalification letter](https://inlineplastics.com/document/how2recycle-prequalification); [PPC Flex](https://ppcflex.com/blog/benefits-of-joining-the-how2recycle-packaging-program/)
- **A converter ERP vendor already ships a sustainability-at-quote feature through a partner.** ePS's Radius ERP (relaunched as "cloud-first" at Labelexpo Europe, Sept 2025) gives users "real-time carbon impact estimates at the quoting stage" through a CarbonQuota partnership. It also offers "Radius Hub," a 24/7 self-service portal for converters' buyers. — [Labels & Labeling](https://www.labelsandlabeling.com/news/workflow-mis-inspection/eps-unveil-modernized-packaging-software-labelexpo-europe-2025); [ePS press release](https://epssw.com/press-releases/eps-labelexpo-europe-2025)
- **A converter using EPR data tooling (EU).** A 2026 trade article names Constantia Flexibles alongside Recyda as examples of using unified packaging data and automated calculations, including design-stage forecasting of eco-modulated fees. No commercial relationship or price was confirmed. Constantia serves 4,000+ pharma, food and consumer customers. — [Packaging Insights](https://www.packaginginsights.com/news/epr-compliance-packaging-data-technology.html); [Constantia Flexibles](https://www.cflex.com/newsroom/constantia-flexibles-receives-recyclass-technology-approval-for-its-mono-pp-laminate-perpetua/)
- **Converter sentiment (UK/EU, 2026):** converters say EPR cost modulation and the UK Plastic Packaging Tax force "much more detailed data tracking." No percentage was given. — [Cantech](https://www.cantechonline.com/blog/41313/packaging-industry-priorities-for-2026/)
- **Readiness surveys are UK producer-side only and dated:**
  - Ecoveritas (2023): 86% of respondents had not fully set up EPR data collection. — [PrintWeek](https://printweek.com/articles/majority-of-affected-businesses-unprepared-for-epr)
  - The same survey's "unprepared" share is reported inconsistently: 42% per [letsrecycle](https://www.letsrecycle.com/news/survey-highlights-lack-of-preparedness-for-epr/); 42.9% "unprepared but aware" plus 14.3% "unprepared" per [Circular Online](https://www.circularonline.co.uk/?p=63238).
  - Packaging World's 2026 Outlook reader survey covers EPR influence, but no readiness percentage surfaced. — [Packaging World](https://www.packworld.com/leaders-new/business-drivers-specialty/sustainability/video/22962495/sustainability-survey-recyclability-and-regulation-reshape-packaging-choices)

### Inferences
- The revealed pattern is that converters pay for credentials their customers recognize (How2Recycle, certifications) and for ERP features bundled into an existing contract (CarbonQuota in Radius). The EPR content they give customers is free. A separately billed converter EPR SaaS would be a new budget line with no precedent.
- ePS's partner-delivered carbon estimate shows how ERP vendors add sustainability features: through a partner, at the quote stage. That supports an "EPR at quote" integration as a partner feature. It also means the ERP vendor, not the founder, controls access to the customer and the pricing.
- Wherever a converter pays, the purpose is to *defend accounts and win RFQs*. A tool positioned as "compliance" will be compared with free SPDC templates and CAA workbooks.

### Gaps
- PIQET, COMPASS/Trayak and Specright supplier-seat pricing, and whether converters buy them, were not found; all are quote-only or unsearched.
- No US survey (FPA, PRINTING United, AICC or TLMI) on converter EPR budgets or request volumes was found. The FPA State of the Industry report may contain one but is not public.
- The How2Recycle converter fee amount is not public.

## 2. EPR software business models and funding (2024–2026); how free calculators make money

### Takeaway
Venture money in this category is small and old. The biggest US-relevant players last raised in 2021 (Specright, ~$42M total) and 2023 (rePurpose, ~$24M total). Recyda raised a €6.3M Series A in Oct 2024. **No 2025–2026 rounds or acquisitions were found for any EPR software vendor.** Pricing has split into an enterprise, quote-only tier and a new crop of self-serve Shopify apps priced from $99 to $9,588 a year (Aug–Oct 2026). Free calculators work mainly as lead generation for paid compliance services or for the supplier's own packaging sales.

### Inferences
- **How the free calculators make money.** rePurpose gates its calculator behind a demo, so the calculator is lead generation for quote-priced services. Supplier calculators such as Paper Tube Co. (prior round) sell the supplier's own packaging. EPR Atlas's model is unknown; it may be content or lead generation. None is a standalone revenue line. This confirms that "fee math" carries no price.
- **Price anchors for the US SMB/mid-market brand side are now public:**
  - $99 a year for a self-serve file generator;
  - $1.2k to $3.6k a year for self-file or expert-reviewed;
  - about $9.6k a year for fully managed.

  These are list prices from an app with no reviews, not proven willingness to pay. Enterprise vendors (rePurpose, Specright, Lorax) price by quote.
- No 2025–2026 VC rounds after the US programs went live suggests investors see this as a compliance-services niche, not a venture-scale software market. That fits a capital-light founder, but it also signals a thin exit market (see §7).

## 3. Producer counts: the brand-side pivot market

### Takeaway
California is the volume driver. One legal alert estimates **about 5,700 covered producers** had to register with CAA or comply independently by June 1, 2026, and **"nearly half" had deferred registration** pending the final rules. Earlier CAA counts were above 1,000 signed participant agreements in Feb 2025 and above 2,300 registrations in about early 2025 (prior round). No authoritative 2026 total for CAA, Oregon or Colorado was found. Small-producer exemptions are low ($1M California in-state sales; about $5M global revenue in Oregon and Colorado). That leaves a large population of small and mid-size brands just above the line, whose fees are often tiny, such as Oregon's lowest flat-fee tier of $700 in 2027.

### Cited Findings
- **California:**
  - About 5,700 covered producers had to register with CAA or choose independent compliance through CalRecycle by June 1, 2026. CAA data indicated "nearly half of the state's producers deferred registration pending the finalization of these rules." — [Mayer Brown, June 1, 2026](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/06/californias-sb-54-epr-regulations-take-effect-key-deadlines-and-compliance-obligations-for-producers); [JD Supra](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/california-finalizes-long-awaited-epr-4006098/); [PackagingLaw.com](https://www.packaginglaw.com/index.php/news/california-finalizes-landmark-packaging-rules-producers-face-june-1-registration-deadline) (attribution approximate among these three)
  - Small-producer exemption: under $1M in-state gross sales, and the exemption must be applied for. — prior round: [H&K, May 2026](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/05/californias-final-epr-regulations-now-in-effect)
- **CAA across states:**
  - "Over 1000 producers" were covered by Participant Producer Agreements in a Feb 11, 2025 CAA update. — [CAA webinar 301](https://circularactionalliance.org/producer-webinars/301-feb-11) (attribution approximate)
  - Producers carry no obligations or fees until both the participant agreement and the state addendum are signed. — [CAA webinar 102](https://circularactionalliance.org/webinar-archives/v/102-registrations-agreements-with-circular-action-alliance)
  - Prior round: more than 2,300 registered (about early 2025, 93% obligated in Oregon); "thousands" per CAA testimony in Jan 2026; Oregon DEQ status lists named 304 producers (Apr 2026) and 198 (Sept 2026), which look like compliance-status or enforcement lists. — [Packaging Dive](https://www.packagingdive.com/news/oregon-recycling-modernization-act-producers-data-reporting/742895/); [EPR Atlas](https://epratlas.com/)
- **Oregon and Colorado exemptions:** Oregon exempts producers under 1 ton or under $5M global revenue; Colorado under 1 ton or under $5.63M (CPI-adjusted). Oregon penalties run up to $25,000 per day (since July 1, 2025). CAA is effectively the only PRO. — [Beancount small-business EPR guide, Sept 27, 2026](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/09/27/packaging-epr-laws-small-business-producer-pro-guide); [ALL4 slides, June 9, 2026](https://www.all4inc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Unpacking-EPR-Packaging-Laws-Basics-6-9-2026.pdf) (attribution approximate; thresholds flagged "approximate" by the source)
- **Fee scale at the small end:** Oregon's lowest-tier flat fee fell from $1,200 (2026) to $700 (2027). — prior round: [CAA OR 2027 schedule](https://circularactionalliance.org/s/OR-2027FeeSchedule_100126.pdf)
- **Fee scale at the large end:** EY's early estimates put EPR fees at "as much as 1% of sales per jurisdiction." — [EY, 2026](https://www.ey.com/en_us/insights/sustainability/us-packaging-epr-in-2026-costs-and-compliance)
- **Consultant and service-bureau price anchors:**
  - **US:** Packledger lists $1,188, $3,588 and $9,588 a year (see §2). A rePurpose customer cites $40k–$120k a year avoided in consultant fees or headcount (vendor-published). Search found no published US per-report consultant price.
  - **UK analog, registration fees:** £1,216 registering directly, or £631 when a compliance scheme registers on the producer's behalf. — [GOV.UK small-producer guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/epr-for-packaging-what-you-must-do-as-a-small-producer)
  - **UK analog, small producers:** defined as above £1M turnover and handling more than 25 t, up to 50 t of packaging (or up to £2M turnover in one test). They must collect and report data but are not yet liable for waste-management fees, per Valpak. — [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/epr-for-packaging-what-you-must-do-as-a-small-producer); [Valpak](https://www.valpak.co.uk/what-do-i-pay-for-understanding-producer-obligations-under-the-uks-new-packaging-extended-producer-responsibility-regulations/)
  - **UK analog, scheme service fees:** neither Valpak nor Ecosurety publishes them; quotes only. — [ComplianceGate](https://compliancegate.com/extended-producer-responsibility-united-kingdom); [Valpak service brochure, Nov 2025](https://www.valpak.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/VAL00008_Service-Brochures-Reducing-packaging-EPR-costs.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Sizing the pivot market (assumption-labeled).** If about 5,700 California producers is right, and 40–60% are small or mid-size brands ($1M CA sales to roughly $50M global revenue) without Specright or Lorax, that is about 2,300–3,400 California SMB/mid-market producers. Most also report in Oregon and Colorado. At $2.4k–$4.8k a year, that is a $5.5M–$16M gross revenue pool for California-led SMB filing services. It is shared with rePurpose, Packledger, Packlog, consultants and in-house staff. *The 40–60% share and the price band are assumptions.*
- The "nearly half deferred" figure and Oregon's recurring producer-status lists of 198–304 companies point to a long tail of laggards, the typical buyer of a done-for-you service. It also suggests many of them are price-sensitive.
- **Size mismatch.** Producers just above the exemption often owe fees in the hundreds of dollars (Oregon's $700 flat tier). They will not pay $3.6k–$9.6k a year to file a $700 obligation unless penalties ($25k a day) or retailer pressure force it. The service sweet spot is therefore mid-size brands ($10M–$250M revenue, multi-SKU, multi-state) where fees run to five or six figures and the work takes weeks. This is an inference.

### Gaps
- No authoritative 2026 count of CAA participants by state, of California registrations after June 1, or of small-producer exemption applications was found.
- The size distribution of producers (how many sit within 2–5× of the thresholds) was not found.
- No published US consultant per-report rates were found, and no Valpak or Ecosurety scheme membership fees.

### Inferences
- A technical integration with Label Traxx is feasible today through its documented API, without permission. A *distribution* deal, where the ERP vendor resells or bundles the product, has no visible program to apply to. It would have to be negotiated with business-development staff at Amtech, ePS and Advantive, which typically takes 6–12+ months for a vendor with no customers (inference).
- ePS's precedent (CarbonQuota as a partner feature) means the ERP vendor would want EPR as a bundled partner feature. Expect the vendor to keep 20–35% of the revenue, or to pay a flat per-install royalty, and to demand proof of accuracy and support capacity. A 1–2 person team with no reference customers is a weak OEM partner (inference).
- **Channel math (assumption-labeled).** Label Traxx's ~500 customers (prior round) × 5–15% attach × $3k–$6k list × 65–80% net to the founder ≈ **$49k–$360k ARR**. Even the upper case is a small business, and it depends on one partner's goodwill.

- The content of "Radius EPR Solutions" is unverified. Next step: ask an ePS rep directly, or fetch the page from another network.
- No installed-base figures were found for Radius, Kiwiplan, Cerm or PrintVis.
- No actual OEM terms from any packaging ERP vendor.
- Whether Amtech or ePS run app marketplaces (e.g., an "Amtech Connect" or "ePS Marketplace") was not confirmed.

## 5. Demand trajectory and legal risk (as of 2026-10-10)

### Takeaway
Demand is locked in for Oregon and Colorado, which are invoicing, and is ramping in California. But California is now under a **multi-state constitutional challenge** (17 states plus NAW, E.D. Cal., filed June 2026). CalRecycle had not approved CAA's plan by early October 2026; the final plan was due to be submitted in October, with approval targeted on or before Jan 1, 2027. NAW's Colorado preliminary-injunction motion was still pending in late Sept 2026. New York failed again in 2026, no new states enacted in 2026, and no federal preemption bill was found. The near-term market is therefore CA, OR and CO, with MD, MN and WA in 2028–2030.

### Cited Findings
- **California plan timeline:**
  - CAA filed its draft plan with the SB 54 advisory board on June 15, 2026.
  - Public comment closed Aug 14, 2026.
  - The final plan is to be submitted to CalRecycle in October 2026.
  - The draft budget runs up to $1.87B for 2027.
  - No report of CalRecycle approval surfaced as of Oct 10, 2026.

## 6. Competitor response: how fast could Specright, rePurpose or ERP vendors ship a converter-facing simulator?

Fast, and in most cases they would only need to recombine features they already ship.
- **Specright** already runs a supplier portal ("Specright Community") and generates multi-state EPR reports from spec records via Lorax.

- **ePS** already ships a partner-delivered, quote-stage sustainability feature.
- **Packledger** showed a three-tier US EPR product can launch within months.

The founder's realistic head start is one selling season.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Estimated time to a "supplier fee view" (inference, not sourced):
  - **Specright**, which has the spec data, the Lorax engine and the supplier portal: a converter-side per-SKU fee view would likely take one to two quarters of product work. ## 9. Verdict: is it worth starting, deploying and selling for an independent, capital-light founder? (go/no-go and kill criteria)

### Takeaway
- **Converter EPR data pack as a standalone, converter-paid SaaS: NO-GO**, unless a fast, cheap paid-pilot test proves otherwise.
  - No converter willingness to pay has been observed across two research rounds.
  - Brand-led free templates and Specright's supplier portal pull the workflow to the brand side.
  - ERP vendors partner rather than buy, and have no open marketplaces.
  - The direct ceiling is about $1M ARR.
- **Best pivot, CONDITIONAL GO:** a service-led, software-assisted EPR filing practice for mid-size brands ($10M–$250M revenue, CA/OR/CO exposure). Converters and co-packers act as *referral partners* who hand over spec data, not as the payer. The converter data pack survives as a free intake format inside it.
  - Conditions: the service must be differentiated from Packledger, rePurpose and Packlog, for example by targeting retail and wholesale food, beverage and household CPG rather than Shopify DTC; it must pre-sell before the May 31, 2027 cycle; and California must not be enjoined.
  - Expected outcome: a $150–500k ARR lifestyle business within about 24 months, not a venture-scale company.

### Cited Findings (decision-critical evidence, recapped from above)
- No converter EPR services or tools were found at Amcor, ProAmpac, Sonoco, Veritiv, TricorBraun or Berlin Packaging; Sonoco's contribution is a free template. — [Packaging Dive](https://www.packagingdive.com/news/epr-reporting-template-sustainable-packaging-data-council/759186/)
- Brand-side price anchors exist at $1,188, $3,588 and $9,588 a year (Packledger, launched Aug 2026) and $99 a year (Packlog). — [Packledger](https://apps.shopify.com/packledger-1); [Packlog](https://apps.shopify.com/packlog-epr-compliance)
- About 5,700 California covered producers, with "nearly half" deferring registration (June 2026). — [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/06/californias-sb-54-epr-regulations-take-effect-key-deadlines-and-compliance-obligations-for-producers) (attribution approximate)
- California faces a 17-state-plus-NAW constitutional suit, and Colorado has a pending preliminary-injunction motion. — [K&L Gates](https://www.klgates.com/Seventeen-States-and-Trade-Association-Challenge-Californias-Extended-Producer-Responsibility-Statute-7-7-2026); [MDM](https://www.mdm.com/?p=436786)
- No 2025–2026 VC rounds or M&A in US EPR software. — [CB Insights (rePurpose)](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/repurpose-global); [CB Insights (Specright)](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/specright/financials)

### Inferences: kill criteria (explicit, testable)

**Converter data pack. Run at most a 60-day test before writing production code:**
1. **Kill** if fewer than 3 of 15 discovery calls with mid-size flex or label converters agree to a *paid* pilot of at least $500 a month, or sign an LOI, within 60 days. Interest without a purchase order does not count.
2. **Kill the OEM path** if none of Amtech (Label Traxx), ePS (Radius) or Advantive (Kiwiplan) signs at least a referral or co-marketing LOI within 90 days, or if ePS confirms that "Radius EPR Solutions" is already a shipped partner module.
3. **Kill** if brands or CAA reject converter-supplied per-1,000-unit data, for example where brands insist all data flows through Specright or SPDC templates. Test this with 3–5 brand sustainability or procurement leads.
4. **Kill** if Specright or rePurpose ships a free supplier-side fee view before the founder has 5 paying converters.
5. **Pause** if counsel concludes that a Colorado or California invoice-disclosure rule bars showing EPR cost on B2B quotes. This removes the "EPR line on the quote" feature.

**Pivot: brand filing service:**
1. **Kill** if there are fewer than 10 paying clients at $1,500 a year or more by May 31, 2027, or fewer than 25 by Sept 30, 2027.
2. **Kill or reprice** if delivery exceeds 15 hours per client per cycle on median, or gross margin falls below 60%.
3. **Kill** if blended CAC exceeds 12 months of ARPA after the first 20 clients.
4. **Pause California expansion** if a preliminary injunction issues in *Nebraska v. Heller* or CalRecycle does not approve the CAA plan by about Q1 2027. Oregon and Colorado alone must still cover costs.
5. **Kill** if CAA or the states release free tooling that auto-generates compliant reports from a SKU list, or if $99-a-year tools match expert-reviewed accuracy and push market prices below $1,000 a year.

### Gaps
- Willingness to pay on both paths rests on vendor list prices and assumptions, not on customer interviews. The kill-criteria tests above are the cheapest way to close that gap.
- California approval and court outcomes due Q4 2026–Q1 2027 could swing the verdict either way.
