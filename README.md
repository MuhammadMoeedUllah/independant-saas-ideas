# Independent SaaS ideas

Research on SaaS ideas built around new US state-level compliance deadlines.

## Reports

1. [State compliance SaaS business feasibility](reports/State%20compliance%20SaaS%20business%20feasibility.md) (Oct 10, 2026, **latest**). Is each idea worth starting, deploying and selling for an independent, capital-light founder? Covers market size, pricing, competitor traction, sales motion and CAC, cost to start (SOC 2, insurance, legal risk), unit economics and break-even, retention, regulatory risk and exit, plus dated kill criteria and a 90-day plan. Supersedes the first report wherever they differ.
2. [State compliance SaaS deep dive](reports/State%20compliance%20SaaS%20deep%20dive.md) (Oct 9, 2026). First pass: competitors, business feasibility, product feasibility and launch plan for each idea.

## Current verdicts

| Idea | Verdict | Best founder fit | Key constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · California DROP / Delete Act compliance ops for data brokers | Conditional go | One technical founder (no-go for two salaries from California alone) | Market size: ~14 vendors split a ~$2.3M California pool; base case ~$290k ARR at 24 months |
| 2 · Pay-transparency cure-clock and posting provenance | Conditional go (narrow) | A founder who sells to employment counsel, or a duo with a SOC 2 budget | Sales velocity: $12–20k contracts, ~16 customers to break even solo |
| 4 · PFAS state rules engine + checkout block + evidence vault | Conditional go (rules + vault); no-go on the certificate network first | Lean Shopify-native solo, ideally with brand relationships | Merchant budgets: checkout app tops out near $0.1–0.3M ARR without a brand tier |
| 5 · Packaging EPR | Converter data pack: no-go. Done-for-you filing for mid-size brands: conditional go | Services- or operations-minded founder | Delivery capacity; Packledger already prices filing at $1.2k–9.6k/yr |

Main decision date in the report: **January 31, 2027**, by when DROP needs 5 paying customers or 10 signed letters of intent and pay transparency needs 3 paid design partners or litigation-support matters.
